Due to the multi-gigabyte scale of raw video frames and high-resolution aerial datasets,
the verified download mirrors, academic citations, and preprocessing manifests are
indexed below for evaluation and reproducibility:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. VisDrone2021-DET & VisDrone-MOT (UAV Aerial Object Detection & Tracking)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: 10,209 high-resolution aerial images and 288 video clips captured by
  various drone platforms at altitudes from 10m to 120m AGL across 14 cities in China.
- Classes: Pedestrian, Person, Car, Van, Bus, Truck, Motor, Bicycle, Awning-Tricycle.
- Access URL: https://github.com/VisDrone/VisDrone-Dataset
- Official Portal: http://aiskyeye.com/
- Primary Task: Training and evaluating Aerial Detector SAHI multi-scale slicing heads.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. UAVDT (UAV Detection and Tracking Benchmark)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Over 80,000 aerial video frames recorded across diverse flight
  conditions (gimbal depression from -30 deg to -90 deg nadir, varied weather, altitudes).
- Access URL: https://sites.google.com/site/daviddo0323/projects/uavdt
- Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-01252-6_29
- Primary Task: Verifying 4D ray-cast photogrammetric altitude/pitch compensation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CrowdHuman & WiderPerson Benchmark (High-Density Crowd Dynamics)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Over 24,000 images with dense annotations (mean 23 persons per image)
  exhibiting extreme occlusion, overlapping silhouettes, and tight crowd packing.
- Access URL: https://www.crowdhuman.net/
- Access URL (WiderPerson): http://www.skicyyu.org/WiderPerson/
- Primary Task: Calibration of velocity divergence flux (div v) and density barometers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. InsightFace / ArcFace Biometric Surveillance Benchmark (512-D Embeddings)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Deep additive angular margin benchmark spanning over 5.8 million identities
  used for training 512-D hypersphere embedding manifolds robust to unconstrained pose.
- Access URL: https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface
- Primary Task: Facial vector indexing, 1:N cosine query calibration, and oblique unwarping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RWF-2000 & UCF-Crime (Real-World Violence & Altercation Anomaly Benchmark)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: 2,000 real-world surveillance video clips of violent altercations,
  physical brawls, and non-violent crowd interactions captured under surveillance angles.
- Access URL: https://github.com/tiansang-bingtian/RWF2000-Dataset
- Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ICPR48806.2021.9412197
- Primary Task: Kinetic interaction graph and relative acceleration altercation classifier.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CityFlow / AI City Challenge MTMC Tracking Benchmark
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Multi-camera multi-target tracking across overlapping and non-overlapping
  camera networks with ground-truth GPS camera extrinsics and road topology maps.
- Access URL: https://www.aicitychallenge.org/
- Primary Task: Cross-Camera Re-ID Handover evaluation connecting UAVs and fixed CCTVs.
